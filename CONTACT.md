# Copyright & Takedown Requests

Hayeon Archive is an unofficial fan-made archival project created for documentation and educational purposes.

If you are a copyright owner, an authorized representative, or believe that content on this website should be removed or corrected, please contact us by email with the following information:

* Your name or organization
* Your relationship to the copyrighted work
* A description of the material in question
* The URL(s) where the material appears
* The reason for your request

Reasonable requests will be reviewed promptly, and appropriate action will be taken where necessary.
