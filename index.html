About Hayeon Archive

Hayeon Archive is a fan-made project dedicated to preserving Jeong Hayeon's public content in one organized place.

The goal of this archive is to make photos, videos, translated Fromm conversations, and future content easy to browse while remaining lightweight, responsive, and accessible across desktop and mobile devices.

Every page is designed with long-term preservation in mind, allowing the archive to continue growing without sacrificing speed or usability.
