⭐ Fun Facts

Interesting facts and milestones about Jeong HaYeon.

⬡

Hexagon

During a profile activity, Hayeon accidentally called a pentagon a "hexagon." Fans loved the moment, and "Hexagon" naturally became the nickname for her personal fandom.

🎨

Artist

Hayeon created the artwork for the 2025 April Fool's Day Objekt (357Z).

🎤

Whistle Register

She performed the whistle notes in Just Do It after Joobin encouraged her to record them.

🎓

Education

HaYeon attended Samhyeon Elementary School, Minrak Middle School, and Graduated from Lila Art High School on February 11, 2026.

🎭

Family

Hayeon is the niece of South Korean actor Im Hyung-joon, known for the Marrying the Mafia film series.

💃

Training

Before debuting, Hayeon trained at FLAT9 Dance & Vocal Academy, alongside fellow tripleS members Dahyun and Joobin.

🌟

Childhood Dream

She admired Chaeyeon after watching Tok!Tok! Boni, Hani as a child. Years later they debuted together in tripleS.

👨‍👩‍👧

Family

HaYeon was born in Pyeongchon-dong, Dongan-gu, Anyang, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea on August 1, 2007. Her family consists of her parents and one older brother (born 2004)

🐬

Special Talent

One of her specialties is singing extremely high notes, which she jokingly describes as sounding like a dolphin.
